Privacy Policy — Boardroom Director

Last updated: 7 October 2026
App: Boardroom Director (mobile game)
Data controller / Provider: Can Behran Kankul
Contact: avelio.info@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how your data is handled when you use the Boardroom Director app (the "Game"). By downloading and using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts, no sign-up and no login.
  • We do not collect personal information such as your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only locally on your device and are not sent to our servers (there is no cloud backup).
  • The Game shows third-party ads (Google AdMob). The ad network may process certain device data to serve ads.

2. Data We Collect

As the Provider, we do not directly collect or store any personal data. The Game has no server under our control.

Data kept locally on your device (stays on your device only):

  • Game save (career progress, club/manager choices, scores)
  • Preferences (language, sound, animation setting)

This data is stored in your device's local storage (localStorage) and is not transmitted to us.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the Game free, it uses the Google AdMob advertising network. AdMob may process data such as the following to serve, cap, measure and (where permitted) personalize ads:

  • Advertising identifier (iOS: IDFA / Android: Advertising ID)
  • IP address, approximate location (country/city level)
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Ad interaction data

This processing is governed by Google's privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses advertising data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent & Tracking Transparency

  • iOS: The app requests tracking permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. If you decline, you will be shown non-personalized ads.
  • Europe (EEA/UK): Your GDPR consent is requested via Google's consent mechanism (UMP). Depending on your choice, you will see personalized or non-personalized ads.

4. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child's data has been processed, contact us at avelio.info@gmail.com.

5. Data Retention

Your local game data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time by:

  • In-game Settings > Reset Save
  • Uninstalling the app

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR and Türkiye's KVKK (where applicable) you have rights such as access, rectification, erasure and objection. Because we as the Provider hold no personal data about you, these requests are mostly limited to the local data on your device (see Section 5). For data processed by the ad network you may need to contact Google. For any question: avelio.info@gmail.com.

7. Security

Your local data is protected by your device's operating-system security mechanisms. Please note that no transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is published on this page; the "Last updated" date changes for material updates.

9. Contact
