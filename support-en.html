Common questions

  • I lost my progress. The game keeps a single save on your device only — there is no cloud backup or account. Reinstalling, switching devices, or "Reset Save" erases it.
  • How do I reset / delete my data? In the game go to Settings > Reset Save, or simply uninstall the app. All local data is removed.
  • About ads. The game is free and supported by ads (Google AdMob). On first launch you can choose your tracking/consent preference; you can change it later in your device settings.
  • No account needed. The game does not require sign-up, login, or any personal information.
